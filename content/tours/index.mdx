## Walking Tours

Explore the Texas A&M campus through curated walking routes. Each tour follows a path through buildings connected by era, theme, or location — with archival photographs and historical context at every stop.

### Historic Core

A one-mile loop through the buildings that defined Texas A&M from its founding. Beginning at the site of Old Main — the university's first structure, built in 1876 and destroyed by fire in 1912 — the route passes through ten stops spanning nearly a century of campus history: dormitories and dining halls, a library, a stadium, and a tree planted before most of them existed.
